Our Story

Born from
Passion, Built with Community

Lucho's is a proudly women-owned, Latino-owned restaurant bringing authentic Mexican flavors to the heart of San Francisco.

What started as a passion for food and a love for feeding people has grown into a local favorite — a place where every guest walks in as a stranger and leaves as family.

We don't just serve brunch. We share culture, we share warmth, and we share a table with anyone who walks through our door.
