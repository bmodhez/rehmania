Our Story

Born in the Lanes
of Dongri

Located in the historic heart of Dongri, Rehmania Restaurant — also known as Al Rehmani — has been feeding Mumbai's soul for years. What began as a modest eatery with a passion for authentic Mughlai food has grown into a beloved institution.

Our kitchen runs on recipes that haven't changed — because why fix what's already perfect? From our legendary seekh parathas to rich chicken gravies, every plate tells the story of a family's love for bold, honest food.

"Not just food. A piece of Mumbai's identity, served fresh every night."
